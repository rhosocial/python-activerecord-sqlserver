Declare the SQL Server capability probes from measurement rather than inheriting core's defaults, and make them load-bearing. The sequence probes answer 2012+ for `supports_sequence`, `supports_alter_sequence` and every option the engine spells, with `supports_sequence_if_exists` at 2016+ (the deleted local `DROP SEQUENCE` renderer had gated `IF EXISTS` on 2012) and `supports_alter_sequence_start` answering `False` from its own ALTER-side question — `ALTER SEQUENCE ... START WITH` is rejected with error 11710 and `RESTART WITH` is the engine's spelling, while `ORDER` and `OWNED BY` have no spelling at all. The identity probes declare `IDENTITY(seed, increment)` (`supports_identity_column` and start/increment `True`) and answer `False` for `ALWAYS`, `MINVALUE`/`MAXVALUE`/`CYCLE`/`ORDER`/`CACHE` — `ALWAYS` had been silently downgraded to the bare property and the options dropped — with `supports_auto_increment_column` `False`. The four master probes are measured too: `supports_truncate` `True`, `supports_with_data_clause` `False`, `supports_materialized_cte` `False` and `supports_transaction_wait` `False`, whose probing trap is that `BEGIN TRANSACTION WAIT` is accepted only because the server reads `WAIT` as the transaction name.
