Add client-certificate (mutual TLS) support to the ODBC connection string. `SQLServerConnectionConfig` could only express server-side encryption via `encrypt` and `trust_server_certificate`; there was no way to present a client certificate. The new `client_certificate`, `client_key`, `client_key_password` and `host_name_in_certificate` fields are emitted as the `ClientCertificate`, `ClientKey`, `ClientKeyPassword` and `HostNameInCertificate` ODBC keywords by `build_connection_string()`, and are accepted as backend kwargs. `validate_config()` now rejects a `client_key` supplied without a `client_certificate`, so the mismatch surfaces as a clear error instead of an opaque driver failure.
