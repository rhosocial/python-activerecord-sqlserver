Fixed `parse_partition_state` and `get_partition_state` (sync and async) to answer an empty `PartitionState` whose `scheme` is `None` for an unpartitioned table instead of `None`, matching core's non-optional `TableState.partitioning` contract. `get_table_state` therefore always carries a `PartitionState`, and the introspection docs no longer describe `None` as the neutral answer.
