Follow core's protocol package split: the dialect now declares `NamespaceSupport` plus one `*ObjectSupport` protocol per catalogue kind and one support protocol per DDL statement (`CreateTableSupport`/`DropTableSupport`/`AlterTableSupport`, `CreateSchemaSupport`/`DropSchemaSupport`, `CreateIndexSupport`/`DropIndexSupport`/`FulltextIndexSupport`, `CreateTypeSupport`/`DropTypeSupport`), with `SQLServerTableSupport` deriving from `TableObjectSupport` and `SQLServerUserDefinedTypeSupport` from `CreateTypeSupport`/`DropTypeSupport`; this backend's protocols and its CLI introspection have moved off the old `TableSupport`/`SchemaSupport`/`IndexSupport`/`FunctionSupport`/`TriggerSupport`/`SequenceSupport`/`ViewSupport` names, so custom subclasses that referenced them must move to the new ones. The local `CREATE SEQUENCE`/`DROP SEQUENCE` renderer overrides are deleted in favour of core's shared probe-gated renderers and `format_next_value_for` now takes `SQLServerNextValueForExpression` instead of `Any`; `.claude/backend_development.md` records the protocol-to-mixin mapping, and CI keeps core pinned to `release/v1.0.0.dev30` while the testsuite installs from its round branch.
